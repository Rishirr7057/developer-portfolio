How I build.

I start with the business process, then work through the data and integrations it depends on.

Start with the workflow

I map the steps, the people involved, and the records each step changes. In WeaveTrack, that meant linking production orders and job cards to inventory and invoicing.

Protect the business record

I consider who can change a record, which inputs need validation, and which updates belong together. WeaveTrack uses permissions and transactions, while See U Beauty Salon validates appointment slots.

Verify data from external systems

Payment gateways and biometric devices send events the application has to interpret. Discover Kashi verifies payment callbacks, and GymPilot maps device punches to members and checks for duplicates.
